# Current model-credit promotion

The GitHub README describes a current 4YI promotion in which eligible purchases receive 100% bonus model credit.

## How the example works

- A $100 qualifying purchase receives $100 of purchased credit plus $100 of promotional bonus credit.
- The resulting usable balance is $200 of model credit.
- Published per-token model rates do not change.
- An “effective cash outlay” example divides the published rate by two because the promotion doubles the usable credit purchased with the same cash payment.

For example, a model with a published output rate of $30 per 1M tokens still consumes $30 of model credit. With 2× promotional credit, obtaining that $30 of usable credit requires an effective $15 cash outlay.

The live offer controls eligibility, start and end times, supported payment methods, participating models, credit validity, consumption order, refund treatment, account limits, and exchange rates. Promotional credit may be non-refundable and may expire.
